| TP032 Artificial Intelligence | TP032 | Artificial Intelligence | TD003 Data & Information | Artificial Intelligence technologies are computing systems, algorithms, and logic-based techniques able to perform a variety of advanced functions and solve cognitive problems in ways that imitate the capabilities of the human mind. | Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies are evolving very rapidly. The HERM representation of AI therefore favours well-established over cutting-edge technology components, and attempts to maintain consistent levelling with other areas of the TRM. Additionally, the TRM contains only technologies, and the application of those technologies to furnish specific AI techniques may be represented by TRM pattern cards or by fully-assembled entries in the ARM. |
| TP014 Data Integration | TP014 | Data Integration | TD003 Data & Information | Data Integration is the technology capability to support the exchange of data between disparate business systems. |  |
| TP034 Physical Estate | TP034 | Physical Estate | TD004 Infrastructure | Physical Estate technologies control and manage the dynamic characteristics of an institution’s built environment. | Physical Estate technologies are interconnected hardware systems specialised for purposes including building automation (e.g., heating, ventilation, and air conditioning; energy and resource consumption), life safety (e.g., fire prevention), physical security (e.g., access control), and user-facing physical assets (e.g., meeting-room booking panels, and in-room audio-visual equipment). |
| TP030 Technical Equipment | TP030 | Technical Equipment | TD004 Infrastructure | Technical Equipment technologies represent specialised instrumentation, machines, things, and sensors that hold particular significance to a specific institution. | There will be many and varied examples of Technical Equipment that is involved in significant ways to enable an institution's activities throughout research and innovation, learning and teaching, partnership and engagement, and operations and estates. Technical Equipment are further categorised as being either Operational Technology or Instrumentation. |
| TP028 Advanced Computation | TP028 | Advanced Computation | TD004 Infrastructure | Advanced Computation technologies use non-traditional architectures to meet specialised computational requirements. | Advanced Computation technologies are typically designed to tackle complex problems more efficiently or enable previously-difficult challenges to be attempted. The field of research computing often makes use of Advanced Computation technologies in one form or another. |
| TP016 Storage | TP016 | Storage | TD004 Infrastructure | Storage technologies provide the capacity and access options to hold data. |  |
| TP017 Network | TP017 | Network | TD004 Infrastructure | Network technologies enable data exchange between interconnected devices. |  |

| TP031 Endpoint Security | TP031 | Endpoint Security | TD005 Cybersecurity | Endpoint Security technologies protect individual network-connected devices against unauthorised access, malware, and other threats. | Devices considered to be endpoints typically include laptops, desktops, mobile phones, tablets, multi-function devices, Internet of Things devices, and point-of-sale systems. The overall practice of securing endpoints draws upon adjacent technology elements, including Firewall, Configuration Management, SIEM, TDR, and UEBA. |
| TP006 Infrastructure Security | TP006 | Infrastructure Security | TD005 Cybersecurity | Infrastructure Security technologies protect the physical and logical components that deliver information-and-communication-technology services. | Infrastructure Security technologies are designed and implemented to assure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of IT infrastructure. |
| TP019 Cybersecurity Intelligence & Response | TP019 | Cybersecurity Intelligence & Response | TD005 Cybersecurity | Cybersecurity Intelligence & Response technologies enable threat and incident detection, response, and prevention. | Cybersecurity Intelligence & Response is scoped to represent only those technology services that are designed and deployed specifically in support of the Cybersecurity mission. The practice of Cybsersecurity draws upon a broad range of technology services and techniques as represented throughout this Technology Reference Model. |
| TP010 Network Security | TP010 | Network Security | TD005 Cybersecurity | Network Security technologies protect communications network resources and traffic from unauthorised access, disruption, and modification. | Network Security technologies cover a broad field that interacts with adjacent capabilities including Infrastructure Security and Endpoint Security. |
| TP011 Technology Administration | TP011 | Technology Administration | TD006 Technology Operation | Technology Administration technologies enable the provision, configuration, and control of technology resources. | Technology Administration typically accept inputs from Technology Observation and use those inputs to effect rules-based adjustments and optimisations to an institution's technology resource estate. |
| TP012 Technology Observation | TP012 | Technology Observation | TD006 Technology Operation | Technology Observation technologies enable monitoring, logging, and alerting of full-stack architecture health and performance. | Technology Observation spans technology disciplines such as performance monitoring at the application, network, and infrastructure layers, and can extend into the observation and reporting of digital experience outcomes. These technologies often provide continuous or real-time observation of technology assets, but may also be deployed for periodic or sampling-based observation. |
| TP035 Identity Governance | TP035 | Identity Governance | TD007 Digital Identity | Identity Governance technologies facilitate the administration and lifecycle management of digital identities and entitlements for individuals associated with an institution. |  |

| TP022 Authentication | TP022 | Authentication | TD007 Digital Identity | Authentication technologies verify the identity of a user, process, or device. |  |
| TP023 Authorisation | TP023 | Authorisation | TD007 Digital Identity | Authorisation technologies control resource access for authenticated individuals. | Authorisation technologies implement either entitlement-based, role-based, adaptively-determined, or individually-assigned privileges to control the access individuals have to systems, resources, and information. |
| TP027 Digital Media | TP027 | Digital Media | TD008 Communication & Collaboration | Digital Media technologies capture, process, display, and interact with audio and visual data. | Digital Media technologies include the capture, manipulation, and processing of traditional sound-and-video assets and of rich-media, three-dimensional, interactive, and extended-reality assets. |
| TP026 Communication | TP026 | Communication | TD008 Communication & Collaboration | Communication technologies facilitate interactions between individuals, groups, and institutions. | Communication technologies can cover a wide scope, and are typically consumed in one form or another by the vast majority of services, applications, and business capabilities. | TP025 Collaboration | TP025 | Collaboration | TD008 Communication & Collaboration | Collaboration technologies provide features and functions that enable people and systems to interact and communicate, work and create together, and share data and documents. |  |
